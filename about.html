Founded on Excellence

Ravvel Builder Sdn Bhd was founded in 2021 and is based in Eco Majestic, Semenyih, Selangor. We specialize in Civil and Mechanical & Electrical (M&E) construction, and have quickly grown into a trusted name in the Malaysian construction industry.

With over 22 years of combined experience in our team, we deliver a wide range of services, including road and infrastructure works, earthworks, bridges, and housing projects. Our experience covers both public and private sector projects across Peninsular Malaysia.

We are committed to delivering high-quality work on time, while maintaining strict standards in planning, quality, and project management. We also value strong client relationships built on trust, transparency, and understanding.

At Ravvel Builder Sdn Bhd, sustainability matters. We aim to use eco-friendly practices wherever possible and continue to improve the way we build for the future.
